FAQ

How do I play? Drag pieces onto the 8×8 board. Fill a row or column to clear it. Clear lines in a row for combos. The game ends when no piece fits.

What are berries? Clear lines with 🍓 blocks to collect berries, then unlock skins and boosters.

I bought "Remove Ads" but still see ads. Open Settings → Restore Purchases.

Will I lose my progress on a new phone? No — progress syncs through your iCloud account.

How do I change my leaderboard name? Settings → Nickname, or the ✏️ button on the Ranking screen.
