🍓 Blockberry — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Blockberry ("the game") is a block puzzle game made by Aelix Apps. This policy explains what data the game uses and why.

1. Data we process

  • Game progress (scores, berries, skins, levels, settings) is stored on your device and synced through your own iCloud account (Apple iCloud Key-Value Storage). We cannot see it.
  • Leaderboards. When you finish a game, the game sends your score, a random player ID generated by the app, and your nickname (random by default, you can change it) to our leaderboard server (Cloudflare). The server stores your country code, which Cloudflare derives from your connection; your IP address is not stored. Nicknames and scores are visible to other players.
  • Advertising. The game shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect device identifiers, the advertising identifier (IDFA, only if you allow tracking), approximate location from IP, and ad interaction data to show and measure ads. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent first, and you can change your choice any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices. See How Google uses data.
  • Purchases ("Remove Ads", berry packs) are processed by Apple. We never receive your payment details.

2. What we don't do

We do not ask for your name, email or contacts, we do not sell personal data, and the game has no account registration.

3. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. Ads are served according to Google's policies.

4. Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes local data. To remove your leaderboard entries, email us with your nickname and we will delete them.
