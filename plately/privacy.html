Plately Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Plately (“the app”) is made by Aelix. We collect as little data as possible and never sell it.

Your data stays yours

Your profile (age, height, weight, goals), meal log, water and weight history are stored on your iPhone. If you are signed in to iCloud, the app also syncs this data to your own private iCloud account (Apple CloudKit) so it is backed up and available on your other devices. We, the developer, cannot access your iCloud data. We do not run user accounts.

What is sent for analysis

When you scan a meal, the photo (downscaled) or your text description is sent over an encrypted connection to our server and processed by Google’s Gemini API to estimate nutrition. It is not linked to your identity, not sold, not used for advertising and not stored by us after the result is returned. An anonymous random install ID is used only to prevent abuse (rate limits). Barcode numbers are looked up in the public Open Food Facts database.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details. You can manage or cancel your subscription in iPhone Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.

Tracking & ads

The app contains no ads and no third-party tracking or analytics SDKs.

Deleting your data

Profile → “Erase all data” deletes everything from your device and your iCloud. Deleting the app removes local data.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Not medical advice

Nutrition values are AI estimates for general wellness and may be inaccurate. Consult a doctor or dietitian before major diet changes.

Политика конфиденциальности Plately

Профиль, дневник питания, вода и вес хранятся на вашем iPhone и, если вы вошли в iCloud, синхронизируются в ваш личный iCloud — у разработчика нет к ним доступа. Фото или описание блюда отправляются по защищённому соединению на наш сервер и обрабатываются Google Gemini только для оценки калорий; они не связываются с вашей личностью, не продаются и не хранятся после ответа. Оплата проходит через Apple. Рекламы и сторонних трекеров нет. Удалить все данные: Профиль → «Стереть все данные». Это не медицинская рекомендация. Связь: aelixapps@gmail.com
