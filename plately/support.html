FAQ

How accurate are the calories? Plately uses AI to estimate portions and nutrition. For the best result, photograph the whole plate from above. You can always adjust portions or tell the AI what to fix (“it was fried in butter”).

I got a new iPhone — where is my data? Sign in to the same iCloud account. Your meals, weight and plan sync automatically.

How do I restore my subscription? Profile → “Restore purchases”. Subscriptions are tied to your Apple ID.

How do I cancel? iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Plately.
